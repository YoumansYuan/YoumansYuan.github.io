01 / About

From open pit to the lab

I work on capital projects at Fording River, one of the largest steelmaking-coal mines in Canada: a tailings dam raise, a haul road reroute, a dust collection system and a pit development study. I first came here for a year-long co-op in drill & blast and dewatering. I worked a 7×7 rotation designing patterns, flying drones over blasts and running a geophysical logging truck, and I helped the blasting and dewatering teams work together on in-pit water management.

Between school terms I worked at the McGill Geomechanics Lab, testing how microwave preheating changes the way kimberlite breaks and how iron ore pellets heat. My capstone was an underground pre-feasibility study for a gold deposit, from stope design to cash flow.

Outside work I'm usually on the water or on a mountain bike. I've raced both since I was a teenager, and I'm a certified snowboard instructor.
