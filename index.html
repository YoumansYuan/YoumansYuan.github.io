01 / About

From the blast pattern to the business case

I'm the project manager and owner's lead for five capital projects at Fording River, one of Canada's largest steelmaking-coal mines. They include a tailings dam raise, a pre-benching mine development program, a new pit, a haul road safety reroute and a plant dust control upgrade.

I started here on a 12-month co-op in drill & blast and dewatering. On a 7×7 rotation I designed around 80 blasts and 20 in-pit dewatering wells, flew drones over blasts and ran gamma-density logging. That field time still shapes how I plan projects with Mine Operations.

At McGill I worked two summers in the Geomechanics Lab on microwave-assisted mineral processing. For my capstone I designed an underground gold mine, from stopes and ramp to production schedule and cash flow.

Outside work I'm usually on the water or on a mountain bike. I've raced both since I was a teenager, and I'm a certified snowboard instructor.

03 / Academic projects

From McGill

Capstone · Fall 2025

Baramiya Gold Project: mine design & pre-feasibility study

An underground gold project of 13.75 Mt at 3.62 g/t, mining about 2,900 t/day over a 13-year mine life. Our study came out at an after-tax NPV of about US$370M and an IRR of about 25%.

  • Stope design & sequencing (my lead): split the orebody into four zones: cut-and-fill in 3 m lifts for Upper, modified Avoca for Central and transverse longhole for Deep, all in Deswik
  • Main ramp (my lead): a footwall ramp at −15% sized for 30 t trucks, with passing bays and space for substations, refuge stations and sumps
  • Productivity model: LHD and truck cycle times, efficiency, utilization and availability, used to set zone production rates and the life-of-mine schedule
  • Geotechnical: ground stability and pillar design in Rocscience RS2 and RS3
  • Report sections I wrote: environmental impact assessment, social licence, SWOT, and conclusions and recommendations
  • Deswik CAD
  • Deswik Stope Optimizer
  • RS2 / RS3
  • Excel
